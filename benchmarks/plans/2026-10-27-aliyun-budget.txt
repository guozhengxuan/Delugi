Budget recommendation
---------------------
Reserve CNY 20,000 for the full regional study at exactly 10 and 50 replicas.

its proposed cap is CNY 13,000. These are alternatives, not additive budgets.

If cross-region confirmation is included, allocate a separate CNY 15,000 cap
for 108 targeted WAN trials. Conditional estimates at 25% and 50% network
utilization are CNY 4,388 and CNY 6,600, including reserve. The conservative
all-public, full-utilization estimate is CNY 13,568. Thus the proposed combined
cap is CNY 35,000 for the full regional study plus this targeted WAN study.
Repeating the entire regional matrix across the WAN is outside this estimate.

Opt-Mempool, Simple-IT Opt-Dispersed, original Autobahn, and Mysticeti-C.

The counts below bound the proposed experiments; they do not imply that the

How much work is budgeted
------------------------
Formal run: 30 s warmup + 300 s measurement + 60 s drain, followed by up to
210 s for reset, evidence collection and cleanup: 10 minutes total per trial.
Short qualification/tuning runs use 5-minute slots and shorter measurement.
Formal comparisons use three repetitions. Tuning uses one discovery pass;
it chooses settings before the repeated formal runs.

Allocate 30 minutes per fleet session for boot, artifact publication,
calibration and final cleanup, plus at most 5.5 hours of trial slots. A final
partial session is released early. Reuse a fleet sequentially across protocols,
interleave/randomize protocol order, and reset stores between trials. Do not
keep 50 hosts running during a 10-host phase. At most 50 validators run at once;
the build stage precedes fleet allocation.

The 10-minute slot assumes at most 0.1 GiB of compressed exported evidence per
host per trial and sufficient parallel collector capacity. Exporting 0.1 GiB
at 5 Mbps takes about 172 seconds before overhead. Qualification must measure
archive sizes, effective export speed and cleanup time. If the allowance is
exceeded, reprice or improve the collection path before the large sweep;
preserve the required correctness/cohort evidence. No quiet truncation of logs.

Except for the stated exceptions, each family includes both n=10 and n=50.
There is no n=20 campaign. The n=4 runs are qualification only, not a paper
scale point. Specifically:

* Qualification: six implementations x two cases x three repeats at n=4,
  then six x two x one qualification pass at each formal size. The large
  performance matrix itself is repeated; this short gate is not its substitute.
* Tuning: six implementations x six candidate configurations x two rates per
  size. This is a bounded candidate set, not every batching/proposal Cartesian
  product. Freeze the selected effective configuration before formal runs.
* Healthy: six implementations x eight offered-load points x three repeats
  per size. Bracket the frontier within these eight points; extra refinement
  or repetitions draw on the reserve rather than an unbounded adaptive sweep.
* Missing-data/leader faults: six implementations x six scenarios x one matched
  load x three repeats per size. Scenarios are (1) a recoverable correct-payload
  gap, (2) Byzantine partial publication with repair refusal, (3) one native
  proposal omission, (4) repeated native omissions within a fixed faulty set,
  (5) maximum tolerated static process silence, and (6) selective Byzantine
  parent publication followed by honest successors. Implement protocol-native
  actors and qualify their semantics. A scenario that does not map to a native
  protocol is marked unsupported; the estimate allocates an upper bound and
  does not license inventing an equivalent attack or fabricating results.
* Network: six x three scenarios x three repeats per size: partial 1% packet
  loss, all-node 1% packet loss, and a finite partition. Exact participants,
  duration and load are fixed before each run and recorded with its seed.
* Payload sizes: two additional useful sizes, 128 and 1,024 bytes, around the
  main 512-byte profile; three load points x three repeats x six per size.
* Timing: eight additional delta/Delta pairs at n=10, two corroborating pairs
  at n=50; six implementations x one load x three repeats. The main profile
  remains added delta=10 ms, Delta=100 ms with reviewed native timer mappings.
  Select admissible sensitivity pairs explicitly; do not change timer semantics.
* Bandwidth: six x two loads x three repeats per size with the injected
  bandwidth limit removed; still private regional traffic, still measured.
* CPU: six x two loads x three repeats at n=10 on the quoted 8-vCPU host.
  Keep the validator memory budget at the declared baseline for CPU attribution.
* Origins: six x two additional origin distributions x three repeats per size.
* Delugi ablation: three additional canonical/paced x bundling off/on settings,
  two loads x three repeats per size; default is already in the healthy matrix.
* Observer controls: two additional minimal/compact/full modes, six
  implementations x one load x three repeats per size; one mode is already
  counted in the main runs. Native Raptr: two loads x three repeats per size.
* Other variants: Autobahn certified tips and both Simple-IT Bracha designs,
  two loads x three repeats per size; subject to qualification and relevance.
* Simple-IT boundary: both optimistic designs, three repeats, n=50, eleven
  silent replicas. This crosses the 40-vote optimistic threshold while leaving
  enough live replicas for the 34-vote high quorum; qualify the actual fallback.

Latency decomposition and resource/traffic figures reuse these trials.
Do not multiply the full matrix by the number of figures. Likewise, this plan
does not cross every fault with every load, payload size and timing pair.

Optional targeted WAN confirmation
----------------------------------
Six implementations x two committee sizes x three loads x three repetitions
= 108 full trials. Reuse qualified binaries and the regional study's selected
settings. Use measured WAN latency and explicitly reviewed timeouts; natural
WAN links cannot share the regional injected one-way 10 ms assumption.

Use Loom's four regions with balanced placements, listed in this order:
  Frankfurt / London / Singapore / Seoul
  n=10: 3 / 3 / 2 / 2 hosts; n=50: 13 / 13 / 12 / 12 hosts.
Each size uses nine trial hours plus one hour of session overhead. Public IPs
use PayByTraffic, with same-region peers routed privately. Quote results:

At uniform peer traffic, the public fraction is (n - local_region_hosts)/(n-1).
Use this only for the utilization scenarios; protocols need not send uniformly.
The conservative scenario instead counts every protocol byte as public.
100 Mbps means aggregate host egress, not 100 Mbps per peer. Budget conversion
uses decimal GB (10^9 bytes), conservatively larger than GiB for the same bytes.

Spending sequence and implementation prerequisites
--------------------------------------------------
1. Finish the local conformance/adapters and reproducible artifact work first.
   The 24-hour build allowance is total billable time across bounded sessions.
2. A CNY 500 initial gate can cover that build allowance, short qualification
   at n=4/10/50 and estimated startup plus contingency (about CNY 376). Only
   reach n=50 after its quorum/proof and resource gates pass. Charge this
   spending against the chosen overall campaign cap, not in addition to it.
3. Run matched Delugi/Raptr healthy sweeps, then extend to the remaining
   qualified counterparts. Stop and release on failed accounting, correctness,
   clock/network calibration or storage/export qualification.
4. Finish the main healthy/fault curves before optional sensitivity variants;
   select the WAN campaign separately after observing actual bytes per trial.

Before execution, extend the reviewed lease/controller capabilities rather
than bypassing existing limits. The current deployment guard accepts at most
31 instances, seven hours, CNY 500, and one fixed 5-Mbps profile. It cannot yet
execute this full plan, the build/CPU profile or traffic-billed WAN leases.
A 50-host six-hour regional lease alone quotes CNY 624.99, or CNY 781.24 with
25% margin. Add explicit per-lease authorization and a persistent aggregate
campaign ledger, reserve cleanup/export costs before scheduling a trial, and
preserve finite auto-release and independent cleanup. No instances or other
paid resources were created while preparing this estimate.
