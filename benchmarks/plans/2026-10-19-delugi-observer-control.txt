Implemented interface and resource bounds
Config.observer_mode defaults to full. Sustained CLI uses --cohort-observer full|compact. Commands receipt, receipt_bitmap and receipt_prefix require quiescence. Bitmap pages are at most4096 bytes; descriptor counts are checked against the trusted configured finite recipe before any bitmap RPC. The schema3 index is at most1MiB; full mode retains its separate schema2 archive and verifier. Headers and finite manifests are Ed25519 authenticated; each bitmap is SHA256 bound with exact byte length, popcount and zero padding. Rust verifies per-origin admission/rejection/readiness/completion relations in bounded streams. These are signed measurement receipts, not a proof that a Byzantine replica truthfully executed its output.

The compact reducer uses O(n*4096) bytes plus the small descriptor/index state; all populations, rejected requests, not-yet-arrived requests and unfinished work remain counted. Runtime retains O(N/8) finite membership per stream and unchanged O(N) application/custody logs. Its footprint is not constant-memory protocol execution. Both modes retain rolling exact-transaction and exact-batch fingerprints, and verify every correct overlapping prefix (including tails beyond a stalled replica). Honest useful credit requires eligibility latched at the origin's original append and membership in the selected correct replica's output. Receipt query/signing/verification occurs after the frozen measured endpoint and is explicitly observer/control work.

Admission and own batching are separate milestones
Useful completion requires the request to have been admitted at its original append event; it does not require the request's own origin batch to have been published. Node.produce may synchronously retry already ordered payloads before returning, and a valid admitted request can appear first in another producer's batch. Both receipt modes therefore latch completed under the common append/admission mutex using admission alone. Full admission-to-output timing is read from the admitted record rather than membership of the origin's pending-batch map. An already completed request later entering its own batch does not recreate a pending timestamp or pending-ready count.

Delugi schema2 metadata explicitly sets batch_ready_precedes_materialization=false: own batch readiness is independently observable and may be later than output or absent at the finite endpoint. Its timestamp remains real and bounded by observation; admission still must precede either stage. Other/native schemas retain their previous default causal stage checks unless they explicitly declare this property. The synchronous produce/retry case, ready-pending counter and stale full pending insertion are covered in new Rust source fixtures; SQL and in-memory reducers agree for absent/later own readiness. Both Rust relation rereads and Python bitmap reduction rehash the bytes they consume against signed descriptors, preventing a post-authentication file change from altering useful counts.
